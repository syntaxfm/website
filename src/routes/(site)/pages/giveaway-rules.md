### 4. Giveaway Period

Most Giveaways happen on the spot, without notice. A Giveaway that is announced ahead of time runs from the announcement until the end time stated in it (the "Giveaway Period"). If no end time is stated, or the Giveaway happens on the spot:

- for a livestream Giveaway, the Giveaway Period ends when the host says entries are closed, or when the livestream ends, whichever comes first; and
- for a Giveaway in a recorded video or community post, the Giveaway Period ends at 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on the seventh (7th) day after the video or post is published.

A Comment Pick doesn't need an announcement. We may pick any comment posted on a Syntax video or livestream within thirty (30) days after it was posted.

Sponsor's clock, together with the timestamps YouTube records, is the official timekeeper for every Giveaway.

Entry is free. Every Giveaway uses one of the formats below. To enter, post a comment (or a live-chat message, for livestreams) on a Syntax video or livestream (an "Entry"). If a Giveaway is announced or the host gives instructions, follow them and enter during the Giveaway Period.

| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Comment Pick**         | Leave a comment. Sometimes there's a prompt from the host; often there isn't.   | Picked by the Syntax team (Section 6)                  |
| **Trivia**               | Answer the trivia question asked by the host                                    | Correct answers, judged under Section 6                |
| **First Correct Answer** | Be the first to post the correct answer (e.g., a number, a word, or a solution) | The first correct Entry received, by YouTube timestamp |

### 6. How Winners Are Picked

Winners are picked by the Syntax team (the "Judges"). We never pick winners at random.

- **Comment Pick:** We pick comments we like, for whatever reason they stand out. It might be a useful tip or correction, kindness toward another community member, something that made us laugh, or just a great comment. There's no scoring formula. Picks are up to the Judges, can happen on any video or livestream, and may happen often, rarely, or never. Most comments and most videos won't have a winner.
- **Trivia:** The first correct answer wins, unless the host says otherwise.
- **First Correct Answer:** The first correct answer wins, based on YouTube's timestamp. The Judges decide what counts as correct.

The host may say how many winners there will be. If not, there's one (or, for Comment Pick, as many or as few as we like). The Judges' decisions are final.

A person may win no more than one (1) prize per thirty (30) day period across all Giveaways.
