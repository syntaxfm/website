Scott and Wes sit down with Michael Arnaldi to dig into Effect v4 and why guardrails matter now that AI agents are writing so much of our code. They discuss going zero dependencies, a 5x smaller bundle, FoldKit, Alchemy, Effect Cluster and workflows, and observability by default.
